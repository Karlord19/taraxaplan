Controls & Manual

Graph

  • Move Canvas: Left-click and drag the empty background.
  • Zoom: Scroll wheel or use the + / - buttons.
  • Center View: Click the "Center" button to fit the entire graph on screen.

Nodes & Actions

  • Create Action: Right-click anywhere on the empty background.
  • Edit Action: Right-click an existing node.
  • Move Node: Left-click and drag a node.
  • Select Multiple: Hold Shift and Left-Click multiple nodes to select them and move them as a group. Left-click the background to drop the selection.

Lines & Knees

  • Add a Knee (Bend Line): Hold Shift and Left-Click anywhere on an existing line.
  • Move Knee: Left-click and drag the knee dot.
  • Delete Knee: Hold Shift and Left-Click the knee dot.

Features & Exports

  • Owners are Critical: The entire export system builds organizers' schedules based on what they "Own". Always ensure your Items, Info, and Actions are assigned to the correct People and Rooms.
  • Filtering: Use the "Filter" button to quickly find overlapping storylines. Click the 🔍 Magnifier icon in any Item/Person edit window to instantly isolate their exact storyline in the graph.
  • Printing: The Export tab structures all data into printable schedules. Use "Export graph" to save a high-resolution, single-page PDF vector of your chart.
